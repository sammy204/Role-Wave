# RoleWave

**RoleWave is a modern job marketplace that helps candidates discover relevant opportunities and helps employers manage their hiring process.**

🌐 **Website:** https://rolewave.cv/

---

## About RoleWave

RoleWave is a modern job marketplace designed to make the hiring process simpler for both job seekers and employers.

For candidates, RoleWave provides a central place to discover job opportunities, explore companies, build a professional profile, and apply for roles.

For employers, RoleWave provides tools to create job listings, manage applications, and organize the hiring process.

The platform is designed with a focus on accessibility, simplicity, responsive design, and a smooth experience across desktop and mobile devices.

---

## What RoleWave Offers

### For Job Seekers

Candidates can use RoleWave to:

- Discover job opportunities
- Search and filter jobs
- Explore companies and job listings
- View detailed job descriptions
- Create and manage a professional profile
- Upload and manage career information
- Apply for jobs
- Track applications
- Manage account and profile settings
- Use RoleWave across desktop and mobile devices

### For Employers

Employers can use RoleWave to:

- Create employer accounts
- Build company profiles
- Publish job opportunities
- Manage job listings
- Review applications
- View candidate information
- Manage the hiring workflow
- Organize recruitment activities

---

## Core Features

### 🔎 Job Discovery

RoleWave provides job discovery tools that allow candidates to browse available opportunities and find roles based on factors such as:

- Job title
- Skills
- Location
- Work arrangement
- Employment type
- Experience level
- Company

---

### 💼 Job Listings

Each job listing can provide important information including:

- Job title
- Company
- Location
- Remote, hybrid, or on-site status
- Employment type
- Salary information where available
- Job description
- Responsibilities
- Requirements
- Skills and technologies
- Application information

---

### 👤 Candidate Profiles

Candidates can create professional profiles containing information such as:

- Personal information
- Professional summary
- Skills
- Work experience
- Education
- Resume information
- Career preferences

The goal is to allow candidates to maintain a reusable professional identity instead of repeatedly entering the same information for every application.

---

### 🏢 Employer Profiles

Employers can establish a company presence on RoleWave with information about their organization and available opportunities.

Company profiles help candidates understand who is hiring before applying.

---

### 📩 Job Applications

Candidates can apply to available roles through the platform and manage their application activity.

RoleWave is designed to make the application process easier to understand and keep candidates informed about their applications.

---

### 📱 Progressive Web App

RoleWave is designed as a modern web application with Progressive Web App capabilities.

This allows the platform to provide an app-like experience while remaining accessible through the web.

The interface is responsive and designed to work across:

- Desktop
- Laptop
- Tablet
- Mobile devices
